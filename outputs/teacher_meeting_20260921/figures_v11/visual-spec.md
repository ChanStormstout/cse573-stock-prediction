# Visual contract

- Canvas: overview 1800 × 700; module 1800 × 640. The landscape composition is fitted into the existing 1280 × 720 slide template.
- Font: Helvetica Neue throughout, matching the slides. Diagram heading 44 px; key modules 30–44 px; annotations 25–32 px. Size is deliberately larger than a dense paper figure because faculty projection is the primary use.
- Blue `#EDF3F9` / navy `#17324D`: standard inputs, encoder, classifier and output.
- Teal `#E7F4F1` / `#006E73`: the grouping and reporting-representation components under study. Teal does not mean trained.
- Solid gray 2.5 px arrows: data. Dashed gray arrow: group membership used by aggregation. No decorative arrows or icons.
- Mild rounded boxes, normal border 2 px, contribution border 2.5 px; no shadows or gradients.
- Panel title above flow; explicit legend lower left; fallback below. Three information streams join before the classifier.
- The large gap between input and classifier in one horizontal band is intentional: the encoder/grouping branches occupy intermediate bands.
- The module figure is intentionally less dense: its five meaningful boxes demonstrate two-stage averaging. No decorative blocks are added to satisfy density heuristics.
- Direct SVG/PDF exports and native PowerPoint primitives derive from the same draw.io coordinates. No generated bitmap replaces the editable computation diagram.
