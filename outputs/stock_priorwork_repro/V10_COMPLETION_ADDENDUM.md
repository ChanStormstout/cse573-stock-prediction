# V10 completion-only recovery

V9 is immutable, partial, and not interpreted as a complete run. Its NEWS-only
evidence may be reused only after an independent integrity audit; its
September-and-later candidate grids are quarantined and never enter selection.
V10 changes no method, grid, canonical association, text, window, target, or
DPRICE definition in response to V9 scores. It refits V9 issued NEWS-only
models only to verify and serialize them, never to reselect them. DPRICE and
joint methods use only V9-authorized March--August issued OOF evidence and the
already preregistered rules. V10 stops if the V9 evidence audit fails.
